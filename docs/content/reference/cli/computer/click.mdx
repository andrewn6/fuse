---
title: fuse computer click
sidebar:
  icon: mouse-pointer
  label: click
description: Left-click at a coordinate on a desktop environment's screen
---

```bash
fuse computer click <id> <x> <y>
fuse computer click fuse-task-1 512 384
```

Coordinates are pixels from the display's top-left corner. The display's
geometry is on [`fuse environment get`](/reference/cli/environment/get)'s
`desktop` row, or take a [screenshot](/reference/cli/computer/screenshot)
first and measure.

With `-o json`, the server's full response is printed, including the base64
PNG screenshot taken after the click settled — the same shape an agent loop
receives.
